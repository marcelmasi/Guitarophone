## Description (max. 4000 characters)

```
Guitarophone turns your phone into an electric guitar.

Hold the phone sideways in front of you, like a guitar, with the screen facing outwards. Strum across the strings on the body with one hand, and hold the strings at the frets on the neck with the other hand. Several fingers can hold strings at the same time, so you can play real chords.

PLAYS LIKE A REAL GUITAR
• Six strings in standard tuning: E A D G B E
• Strum across the strings, or tap a single string to pluck it
• Faster strums sound louder
• Slides, hammer-ons and pull-offs: move a finger along a ringing string
• Lift your finger and the string stops ringing
• String bending: push a held string sideways to bend the note up

THE SOUND OF AN AMP
• Three amp tones: Clean, Crunch and Lead
• The strings are simulated while you play, not played from recordings, so every note rings and fades like a real string
• The sound also plays with the silent switch on

MADE TO FIT YOUR HANDS
• Change the size of the strum area and of the neck, and the number of frets (1 to 12), right on the guitar
• Swap the sides, so you strum on the right
• Show or hide the note names

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no tracking. The app collects no data. The source code is on GitHub: github.com/marcelmasi/Guitarophone
```

## Notes for App Review

```
No login is needed. The app works only in landscape: hold the phone sideways. Tap "Play" on the start screen. Then strum across the strings on the left side and hold strings at the frets on the right side. The gear button in the upper left corner opens the settings (amp tone, string bending, sizes of the guitar).
```
