Strumming near the edges

A swipe that starts right at the top or bottom edge of the screen can open Notification Center or the app switcher. The guitar catches a single swipe, but a second one right after it reaches iOS.

Start your strums a bit away from the edges, or keep your finger on the screen while strumming up and down.

To block these swipes completely, turn on Guided Access in the iOS settings under Accessibility. Then triple-click the side button while playing. Triple-click it again to end Guided Access.
