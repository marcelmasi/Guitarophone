# Privacy Policy

Guitarophone collects no data.

- The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- The app does not connect to the internet.
- The app does not use the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts or any other personal data.
- Your settings (amp tone, sizes of the guitar and so on) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

The developer receives no information about you or about how you use the app. Only if you allowed it in the settings of your device, Apple or Google may give the developer anonymous download numbers and crash reports, as described in their own privacy policies.

Questions: please open an issue at https://github.com/marcelmasi/Guitarophone/issues.
